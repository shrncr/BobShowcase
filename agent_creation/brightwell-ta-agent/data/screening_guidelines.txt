Brightwell Health Talent Acquisition Screening Guidelines

OVERVIEW
These guidelines ensure consistent, objective candidate screening across all recruiters and requisitions.

SCREENING PROCESS
1. Review the job requisition to understand must-haves and nice-to-haves
2. Review the candidate's application, resume, and credentials
3. Compare candidate qualifications against requirements
4. Generate standardized 5-part screening summary
5. Provide clear recommendation for next steps

5-PART SCREENING SUMMARY FORMAT

Part 1: Match Call
- Strong: Meets 80%+ of must-haves, excellent fit
- Possible: Meets 50-79% of must-haves, worth exploring
- No: Meets <50% of must-haves, does not meet minimum requirements

Part 2: Must-Haves Checklist
List each must-have requirement with Yes/No/Unclear status:
- License/Certification: Verify active status and state
- Years of Experience: Compare against requirement
- Unit/Specialty Experience: Confirm relevant background
- Required Certifications: Check for BLS, ACLS, specialty certs
- Education: Verify degree requirements

Part 3: Relevant Experience Summary
1-2 sentences highlighting:
- Most relevant experience for the role
- Key strengths and specializations
- Notable achievements or skills

Part 4: Flags
Identify any issues or missing information:
- License information not provided
- Certification status unclear
- Experience level below requirement
- Critical information missing from application
- Inconsistencies between resume and application

Part 5: Recommended Next Step
- Phone Screen: For Strong and Possible matches
- Pass: For No matches
Include brief rationale for recommendation

HEALTHCARE-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS

Nursing Positions (RN, NP)
- Verify active RN/NP license in California
- Check for required certifications (BLS, ACLS, specialty certs)
- Confirm unit-specific experience (ICU, Med-Surg, Primary Care, etc.)
- BSN preferred for most positions, required for some

Allied Health (PT, OT, RT, etc.)
- Verify active professional license
- Check for specialty certifications
- Confirm clinical experience in relevant setting
- Verify degree requirements (DPT, MOT, etc.)

Clinical Support (MA, Lab Tech, etc.)
- Verify certification (CMA, RMA, MLT, MLS)
- Check for state-specific licenses where required
- Confirm experience in relevant clinical setting
- EHR experience (Epic, Cerner) is valuable

COMMON RED FLAGS
- License information missing or unclear
- Experience significantly below requirement
- No relevant unit/specialty experience
- Required certifications missing
- Gaps in employment not explained
- Inconsistencies in application materials

BEST PRACTICES
- Be objective and data-driven
- Use consistent format for all screenings
- Flag gaps clearly without making assumptions
- Provide actionable recommendations
- Maintain professional, unbiased language
- Document rationale for decisions

QUALITY STANDARDS
- All screenings must use the 5-part format
- Must-haves checklist must be complete
- Flags must be specific and actionable
- Recommendations must include rationale
- Language must be professional and objective

ESCALATION
Escalate to TA leadership when:
- Candidate has unique qualifications not covered by standard criteria
- License or certification status is unclear
- Significant discrepancies in application materials
- High-priority requisition requires expedited review
